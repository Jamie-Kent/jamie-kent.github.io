<h3 id="audio-capture">How to record the internal audio</h3>
<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LSmM5FXzVBg">Video Guide</a>

<br><br>

<p>To do this you need to install a third party app called BlackHole. 
    Steps to complete this are listed below</p>


<b>Download and Install</b>
<ol>
    <li>Download <a href="https://existential.audio/blackhole/?pk_campaign=github&pk_kwd=readme">BlackHole</a> (<a href="https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole">GitHub Link</a>) </li>
    <li>Use a <a href="https://mail.tm/en/">tempmail</a> email address to obtain a download link</li>
    <li>Complete the setup wizard</li>
</ol>

<b>Setup of aggregate device</b> <br>
<ol>
    <li>From spotlight search, find "*Audio MIDI Setup*"</li>
    <li>Click the plus at the bottom right then choose "*Create aggregate device*"</li>
    <li>Check use "*BlackHole 16ch*" and your microphone for voice</li>
    <li>Click the plus again then choose "*Create multi-output device*"</li>
    <li>Check the "*Built-in output*" and the "*BlackHole 16ch*"</li>
    <li>Make sure they're in the correct order, built-in appearing at the top of the list</li>
</ol>

<b>Renaming the audio devices</b>
<ol>
    <li>Rename the aggregate to <i>"Quick time input"</i></li>
    <li>Rename the Multi-Output Device to <i>"Screen recording audio"</i></li>
</ol>

<b>System preferences</b>
<ol>
    <li>Open System Preferences from spotlight search</li>
    <li>Go to Sound>Output>Screen recording audio</li>
</ol>

<b>Quick Time player settings</b>
<ol>
    <li>Open QuickTime Player</li>
    <li>Go to File>Recording>Options>Microphone>QuickTime Input</li>
</ol>

<b>Reverting settings** </b>
<ol>
    <li>Open spotlight search</li>
    <li>Go to Sound>Output>Macbook Air Speakears</li>
</ol>
