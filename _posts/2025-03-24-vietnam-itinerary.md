*   **April 22 (Tuesday): Ha Giang Loop - Day 2**

    *   Morning: Continue towards Dong Van.
    *   Afternoon: Visit attractions along the way.
    *   Evening: Find accommodation in Dong Van.

*   **April 23 (Wednesday): Ha Giang Loop - Day 3/Transfer to Hanoi**

    *   Afternoon (2:00 PM): Complete the Loop, return to Ha Giang City for lunch and meet the private car driver.
    *   Afternoon (2:00PM): Driver Starts towards airport accomodation.
    *   Evening (7:00 PM - 8:00 PM): Arrive at your hotel near Noi Bai International Airport.
